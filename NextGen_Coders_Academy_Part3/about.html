OUR STORY

About NextGen Coders Academy

NextGen Coders Academy helps students and aspiring developers gain practical technology skills. We believe coding should be accessible, engaging and connected to real-world problems.

Our programmes support different stages of learning, from complete beginners to students who want to strengthen their existing programming skills.

OUR PURPOSE

Technology Education With a Practical Focus

We encourage students to learn by doing. Lessons are connected to projects so learners can practise concepts and understand how technology is used outside the classroom.

Our approach focuses on confidence, creativity, problem solving and continuous learning.
