About NextGen Coders Academy

NextGen Coders Academy is a coding academy focused on helping students and aspiring developers gain practical technology skills. We believe that learning to code should be accessible, engaging and connected to real-world problems.

Our programmes are designed for people at different stages of their learning journey, from complete beginners to students who want to develop their existing programming skills.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible and practical coding education in a supportive learning environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to develop a new generation of confident, creative and skilled programmers who can use technology to solve real-world problems.

Our Values

  • Innovation
  • Creativity
  • Continuous learning
  • Teamwork
  • Integrity

Who We Serve

Our academy is suitable for school leavers, university students, young adults, career changers and anyone interested in developing programming and technology skills.
